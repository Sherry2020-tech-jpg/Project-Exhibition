✦ Meditation Protocol

  • Sit at the same time daily — the habit matters more than the length.
  • Two minutes of breathwork before a sit settles the mind faster than willpower.
  • Wandering thoughts are not failure — noticing the wandering IS the practice.
  • Use headphones with the Sound Cosmos for the full binaural effect.
  • Check in before and after — what gets measured gets improved.
  • End every sit with one line in Mind Notes — it anchors the insight.
