✦ Creativity Protocol

  • Quantity breeds quality — aim for volume, judge nothing during generation.
  • Steal from everywhere — combinational creativity is the only kind there is.
  • Constraints are fuel: give the mind a fence and it builds a ladder.
  • Two unrelated ideas touching = the spark of every invention ever made.
  • Capture everything instantly — an unwritten idea evaporates in ninety seconds.
  • Review your vault weekly — old ideas remix into new breakthroughs.
