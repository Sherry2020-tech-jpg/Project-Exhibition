✦ Neuro-Training Protocol

  • Train 10–15 minutes daily, not 2 hours once a week.
  • Sleep 7–9 hours — memory consolidates during deep sleep.
  • Hydrate before sessions; even mild dehydration slows reaction time.
  • Alternate protocols to stimulate different neural circuits.
  • Difficulty should feel challenging but achievable — the "flow zone".
  • Track your dashboard weekly; progress compounds silently.
