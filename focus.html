✦ Focus Protocol

  • One sprint, one target — never multitask inside a sprint.
  • When distracted, don't fight it. Note it in the Vault and glide back.
  • Take the Vigil Test at the same hour daily — scores track your real cognitive rhythm.
  • Phone in another room beats every willpower trick ever invented.
  • Start with 15-minute sprints; grow to 45 as your gravity strengthens.
  • End the day with a one-line shutdown note — it closes the open loops.
